**Agreements:** I agree with the shared rejection of A: static contracts cannot detect retrieval omission or obsolete application. **Disagreement / qualification:** No disagreement with the shared selection of B. I would not describe the now-expanded campaign as exactly 60 baseline runs. R9's six-family shorthand and its evidence-shape totals conflict with the scenario matrix's instruction to give every C2 variant ten seeds. C2 also calls these three variants but separately requires read-only-stale and maintenance-stale outcomes, which require distinct goals/authorization and scoring. With valid-chain, conflict, stale-read-only and stale-maintenance each receiving ten seeds, plus five other families, baseline is 90 runs, and a full paired plus held-out candidate phase adds 180 runs.

**Residual risks and recommended handling:**

1. Executor completeness is still unverified. A recorder could omit a direct read or an early architecture-bearing action and falsely satisfy ordering. Qualify capture using deliberate alternate reads and pre-retrieval output/write probes, not just a normal successful trace. A missing action must yield invalid evidence, never pass.
2. A goal-start snapshot proves currency at that boundary, not throughout a concurrently changing consumer session. Keep fixture sources immutable during the scored run except explicitly scoped index maintenance, or invalidate evidence on an authoritative-source change. Preserve the report's tested-run claim rather than promising production freshness generally.
3. Hash comparisons must cover equivalent content ranges: a decisive retrieved range and its matching authoritative range, or corresponding full-document representations. Do not accidentally reject valid ranged retrieval due to whole-file versus response-envelope hashing, or accept a stale decisive value because another range matched.
4. Existing rule/skill mutation and collection-authority conflicts remain diagnosis targets rather than repaired runtime behavior. The stale and authorization variants should expose them; do not claim their resolution before observed evidence and verified installed amendments.
5. Synthetic small-corpus success and held-out seeds still do not establish every consumer runtime or ordinary-work distribution. Maintain versions, instruction hashes and per-variant counts in the report. Escalate actual incidents under the deferral without waiting for broad matrices.

**Main risk if wrong:** A complete-looking benchmark can still mislead consumers into treating bounded successful runs as continuous prevention of stale or superseded authority. An unverified capture boundary or ambiguous campaign denominator would make that confidence particularly unsafe.

**Own confidence:** High in B over A/C. Medium in practical executor feasibility and eventual success until qualification and live evidence exist. The amended design substantially improves current-canon coverage; it has not yet supplied behavioral proof.
